How to Play

A Race of Fate, Fortune, and Perfect Rolls.

The Goal

Start at the Verdant Threshold. Reach the Golden Gate of Elysium at the top. The first three heroes to arrive place first, second, and third.

Your Turn

  1. Pick a die: D4, D6, or D8.
  2. Roll and walk that many spaces.
  3. Do what the space you land on says.
  4. Next hero.

Only the space you stop on matters. Share spaces freely.

Whoever is in the lead may not take the D8. The front runner walks carefully.

Golden Gates

Land on a stop sign and you stop, even mid-move. Here is where the paths diverge.

On your next turn, pick a die and roll. If you roll its highest face, you may take the Golden Path shortcut. Roll anything else and you’ll go the long way. The fates decide.

Small die, better odds. Big die, better consolation. One attempt per Gate. The leader still cannot pick the D8, even here.

D41 in 4miss: move 1–3
D61 in 6miss: move 1–5
D81 in 8miss: move 1–7

Favor of the Forgotten

Whoever is furthest behind may roll the D12 instead. No measuring — if it is obvious, it counts.

Roll a 1 and the fates take pity: move the single space, then roll again.

Appeal to the Fates

Once per game, roll the D20 instead of moving normally.

Cards

Read it, do it, discard it. Nothing is held or saved.

If a card lands you on a colored space, resolve that too — then stop. Never a third.

Winning

Reach or pass the gold space. No exact roll needed. Step off the trail and claim your place.

If You Are Stuck

The host decides. The host is always right.
